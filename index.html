How It Works

Wash. Correct. Coat.

Every service we offer sits somewhere on this line. We never skip a stage — the protection only lasts if the paint underneath it is prepped right first.

01

Wash & Prep

A full decontamination wash lifts dirt, iron and tar so nothing gets dragged across your paint during the next stage. This is where Stage 1 & Stage 2 detailing live.

Stage 1 · Stage 2
02

Correction

Machine polishing cuts back swirl marks and oxidation to reveal the paint underneath — built into Stage 2 and Full Detail, and finished off with headlight renewal where needed.

Included in Stage 2 & Full Detail
03

Coat & Protect

A wax layer or a professional-grade ceramic coating seals the work in — hydrophobic, weather-resistant, and built to keep that finish looking like day one.

Wax · Ceramic Coating
What We Offer

Services

01

Stage 1 Detail

Maintenance wash & decontamination
▾

The essential refresh. Good for regular upkeep between deeper details, not for a car that needs real work.

  • Foam pre-wash and hand wash
  • Wheel, tyre and arch cleaning
  • Iron and tar decontamination
  • Interior vacuum and wipe-down
  • Tyre dressing and window clean

02

Stage 2 Detail

Enhancement detail
▾

Everything in Stage 1, plus a gloss-enhancing polish for a noticeably deeper shine without a full coating job.

  • Everything in Stage 1
  • Clay bar paint decontamination
  • Machine-applied gloss enhancement
  • Interior deep clean
  • Glass polish, in and out
Enquire About This
03

Full Detail

Complete interior & exterior
▾

The complete reset, inside and out. For cars that need everything addressed in one sitting.

  • Full exterior wash and decontamination
  • Machine polish for gloss and clarity
  • Interior shampoo and steam clean
  • Engine bay clean and dress
  • Trim and plastic restoration
Enquire About This
04

Wax Protection

Coat & protect
▾

A traditional wax layer for a warm, deep shine and a solid layer of short-to-medium term protection. A good entry point between full coating jobs.

  • Surface prep and decontamination
  • Hand or machine-applied wax layer
  • Water-beading protection
  • Deep, warm gloss finish
Enquire About This
05

Ceramic Coating

Coat & protect
▾

The finishing armour that locks the shine in for the long run. Long-term hydrophobic, chemical-resistant protection, best applied once the paint is fully corrected.

  • Surface prep and final decontamination
  • Professional-grade ceramic application
  • Hydrophobic, UV and chemical resistance
  • Deep, long-lasting gloss
Enquire About This
06

Headlight Renewal

Restoration & UV sealant
▾

Oxidised, yellowed lenses restored to clear and sealed against UV so it doesn't come straight back.

  • Wet-sand of the oxidised lens layer
  • Multi-stage machine polish
  • UV-protective sealant application
  • Better night visibility and safety
